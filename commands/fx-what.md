# /fx:fx-what

The owner has lost the thread. Lead with the point, in plain words, then give
four short parts:

- **Asked:** what the owner wanted.
- **Done:** what is finished, and how that is known.
- **Pending:** what is left, in order.
- **Needs you:** each decision or action only the owner can take. "Nothing" is
  an answer.

Use the project's terms from `GLOSSARY.md` (or the older `CONTEXT.md`). When a
build ledger exists under `docs/plans/<slug>/`, read its tail instead of
recalling. Change nothing.
