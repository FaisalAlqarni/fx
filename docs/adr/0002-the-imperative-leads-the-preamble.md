# The imperative leads the preamble

`PREAMBLE.md` opens with the section telling the agent to invoke a lane. That
position is load-bearing and is not a matter of presentation.

Measured, same words and same prompt, five reps each: the imperative at line 56
of 210 fired **0/5**. The imperative first, with the full 210 lines and nothing
removed, fired **5/5**. Moving one section took the lane suite from 5 of 6 to
6 of 6.

**In an injected context, order is force.** The first section survives;
everything after it competes.

## Consequences

- **Nothing gets added above it.** fx once diluted its own most load-bearing
  section by adding good content above it, then read the resulting silence as
  evidence that instruction does not work.
- Audit that position the way you would audit a guard. It fails silently and
  completely, and the failure looks like a description problem somewhere else.
