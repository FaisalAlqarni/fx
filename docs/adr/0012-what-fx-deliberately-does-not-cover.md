# What fx deliberately does not cover

The explicit no's, so they are not re-proposed as oversights.

**No design reference corpus.** `fx-design` carries the calibrated part that
changes behaviour: the convergent looks a model reaches for by default, and the
families to avoid. Palettes, type scales and style vocabularies on disk were
considered and dropped, because the model can already generate a palette. What
it cannot do unprompted is notice it is producing the same page again.

**No motion or animation guidance** beyond the one rule in `fx-design`: motion
nobody triggered is for attention, and one orchestrated moment beats scattered
effects.

**No platform procedure skills.** Android and iOS build procedures were in an
early inventory and removed. They are project facts, so they belong in `repo.md`
under `0003-three-layers-of-knowledge.md`.

**No browser-driven design loop.** `fx-design` says to look at the built result
and screenshot it where the environment allows. Driving a running app, critiquing
and adjusting would mean owning a driver, and that is not in.

**No CI.** Five gates and four suites run only when a human types them, and that
is how a prose gate once reported clean over 117 violations. Recorded as a known
gap rather than a decision, and it is the most likely thing on this page to
change.

## Still undecided

**The `ui-ux-pro-max` corpus.** Roughly 440K of stack-agnostic CSV covering
style vocabularies, product palettes, font pairings and chart forms, inside a
23MB plugin whose remainder emits React and Phosphor icons. `fx-design` shipping
without it makes this less pressing, not moot. Nothing else is open.
