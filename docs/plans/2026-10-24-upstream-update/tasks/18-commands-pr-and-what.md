# 18: `/fx:fx-pr` and `/fx:fx-what` commands

**Status:** ready-for-agent
**Blocked by:** 17
**Phase:** 4: New commands

**What to build:** two commands the owner types, never chosen by a model, so neither enters the lane selection list as a rival.
- **`/fx:fx-pr`** drafts a pull request description the owner can paste, and never pushes, opens or edits a PR. Shape: "What and why", three lines at most; "Changes", one line per area, about seven at most; "Evidence", what ran with its result, always ending with what did not run; "Merge danger", one-way or two-way door and blast radius, two lines. No code, diff or diagram unless the owner asks. It reads `git log --oneline <base>..HEAD` and `git diff --stat <base>...HEAD`, and when the branch came from a plan, its design.md and `state.md` (test evidence per task, `Ruling:` lines). It shows the draft in chat and, when a plan exists, saves it to `docs/plans/<slug>/pr.md`. fx-implement's completion menu option 2 tells the owner to run `/fx:fx-pr` for the body (a lane cannot invoke a user-invoked command).
- **`/fx:fx-what`** re-explains the current state of the session or build in plain words when the owner has lost the thread: what was asked, what was done, what is pending, what needs the owner. It leads with the point, uses the glossary's terms (GLOSSARY.md, or the older CONTEXT.md), and when a build ledger exists reads its tail instead of recalling.
